# The 3D interviewer model

`mpfb.glb` comes from the TalkingHead project (https://github.com/met4citizen/TalkingHead, v1.8.2).
It was made with Blender + MPFB (MakeHuman) and is licensed **CC0**: free for any use, including commercial.

Only CC0 or properly licensed models belong in this folder. Many sample avatars online (Avaturn, Ready Player Me,
Avatar SDK) are non-commercial only, so keep those out of this repository.

To use another model, put the .glb here and change MODEL in `src/lib/avatar.js`. It needs a Mixamo-style
skeleton, the 52 ARKit face shapes and the Oculus viseme shapes (for lip-sync).
